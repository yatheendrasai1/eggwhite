The Article of the Matter

Hindi and Telugu don't have articles at all — which is exactly why "a," "an," "the," and no article are the single most common daily slip in Indian English. The rules are tight. This drill locks them in.

How it works

Part I — Fill the gap. Each sentence has a blank. Choose "a," "an," "the," or "no article" — whichever the sentence actually needs.

Part II — Pick the correct sentence. All four options are the same sentence with only the article changed. Pick the one that's actually right.

Scoring: each item is worth 1 point, 32 total.
