The Corporate Confusion

"Revert back," "discuss about," "kindly do the needful" — the padded phrases that instantly flag an email as non-native. Plus the word pairs that look alike, sound alike, and get swapped constantly: affect/effect, its/it's, loose/lose.

How it works

Part I — Cut the redundancy. Four versions of the same sentence. Pick the one that says it without padding or doubling up.

Part II — Confusable words. Pick the word that actually belongs in the blank.

Scoring: each item is worth 1 point, 30 total.
