The Incorrectly Correct Things

Two habits that sound fine in your head but aren't: putting stative verbs like "know" and "want" into the continuous ("I am knowing"), and pairing the wrong verb with a fixed noun ("do a mistake" instead of "make a mistake").

How it works

Part I — Stative verbs. Pick the correct form of the verb in brackets. Most stative verbs never take the "-ing" form — but a few verbs change meaning and do take it, so read carefully.

Part II — Collocations. All four options are the same sentence with only the word pairing changed. Pick the one that's actually how English is used.
